### Can a verdict be trusted?

- **vérifié:** 61 of 70 were sound when read by hand. The others treat group elements or endomorphisms as commuting numbers, or an inverse image as an inverse.
- **réfuté:** all 10 on these correct book equalities were false alarms, from notation such as Legendre symbols or garbled tables. So it means "read this again", not "this is wrong".
- Two parser fixes came from reading this same sample, and the run was repeated, so this isn't a fully untouched test. Both only make the checker more cautious.
