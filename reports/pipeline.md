Pipeline minus LLM alone: -0.20 (95% CI -0.44 to +0.00). The pipeline did worse.

## Why

- The second opinion withdrew 17 flags: 10 were the planted error on the right line, 6 were false alarms on controls. It cut false alarms, but it threw away more real errors than wrong ones.
- Read after the fact, the judge alone (flags put back) would have found the exact line 0.72 of the time with 0.28 false alarms, both right 0.52. This was not the frozen design, so it is not a test result.
- The computer checked 156 claims on these PDF-extracted textbook solutions and could verify 8 and refute 1: almost no evidence for the judge.
- 4191 tokens per review, against about 1600 for LLM alone.

With 25 exercises, differences under about 0.2 can't be told apart from chance. The test set has now been used twice; any further change is exploratory until new test data exists (real student attempts).
